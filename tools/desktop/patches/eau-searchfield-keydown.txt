Eau swizzled NSSearchField's keyDown:, which NSSearchField inherits:
exchanging the inherited method swapped the superclass's implementation,
and a key sent to a button then recursed between -[NSButton keyDown:]
and -eau_keyDown: until the stack overflowed (LoginWindow crashed on
Return after a wrong password).  Add the method to NSSearchField first,
as the NSButton swizzle does.
