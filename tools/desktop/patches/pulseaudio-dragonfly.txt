FoxynBSD changes to PulseAudio 17.0 (contrib/pulseaudio), whose client
library and utilities (libpulse, pactl, paplay) are built for PulseAudio
applications, which then play through PipeWire's pipewire-pulse:
- credentials over Unix sockets as on FreeBSD (struct cmsgcred,
  SCM_CREDS); DragonFly has no struct ucred nor SCM_CREDENTIALS
- the program's name from sysctl's KERN_PROC_PATHNAME, as on FreeBSD
